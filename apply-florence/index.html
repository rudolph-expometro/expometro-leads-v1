About Me

My name is Rudolph.

After working at leading auction houses and prestigious art galleries, I decided to break the rules of the art market. I was tired of seeing so many talented artists lost online or unable to exhibit beyond their own homes.

Today, my mission is simple: to take art beyond social media and bring it into real life, in front of the public.

I can't wait to discover your work and hopefully meet you in Florence—or through Instagram Live.
